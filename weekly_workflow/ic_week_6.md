<blockquote align="justify">
	The study proposes a reinforcement learning (RL)-based Dynamic 
	Difficulty Adjustment (DDA) approach to maintain player flow in 
	turn-based battle games. Using a SARSA algorithm, the AI opponent 
	adapts its action selection according to the player’s performance 
	rather than modifying game statistics or the environment. Rewards 
	are dynamically adjusted based on consecutive player wins or losses, 
	creating personalized challenges. Evaluation results indicate that 
	the RL agent learns effective battle strategies over time, with a 
	30:70 exploration-to-exploitation ratio achieving the best balance. 
	The authors conclude that RL-driven opponents can improve game flow 
	and provide a foundation for more adaptive AI in complex turn-based 
	games.
